AI Product Intern — Pebble (consumer mobile app)

Pebble is a journaling app with 3M monthly users. We are adding an AI companion that
summarizes your week and suggests reflections.

You will
- Talk to users: run user interviews and usability testing on early AI features.
- Write PRDs for new AI features and keep them updated as we learn.
- Work with designers in Figma and with engineers who build the app in React Native
  and our web dashboard in React.
- Write and iterate on prompts with the team; help define prompt engineering guidelines.
- Track launch KPIs and dig into funnel analysis after each release.

Nice to have
- You have shipped something with an LLM API, even a side project.
- Basic SQL to answer your own product questions.
- Comfort working in Jira and writing clear tickets.
