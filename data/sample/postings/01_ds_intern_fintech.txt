Data Science Intern (Summer 2027) — Ledgerline, a consumer fintech company

About the team
The Risk Analytics team builds the models that decide credit limits for two million
cardholders. You will work with senior data scientists on fraud and credit-risk projects.

What you will do
- Build and validate credit-risk models in Python using pandas and scikit-learn.
- Write SQL against our Snowflake warehouse to assemble training data.
- Run statistical analysis on model performance across customer segments.
- Help design A/B tests for new underwriting rules and read out the results.
- Present findings to the risk committee with clear data visualization.

What we are looking for
- Strong Python. Our whole stack is Python, our notebooks are Python, and our
  model-serving code is Python, so you will write Python every day.
- Coursework in statistics and machine learning.
- Comfort with SQL joins and window functions.
- Bonus: experience with time series forecasting or causal inference.
- Bonus: Git and code review habits.
