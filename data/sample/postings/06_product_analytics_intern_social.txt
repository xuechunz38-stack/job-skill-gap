Product Analytics Intern — Ripple (short-video social app)

Ripple's product analytics team partners with product managers to decide what to
build next. You will join the Feed & Discovery pod.

Responsibilities
- Define and monitor product metrics such as retention, session depth and the
  north star metric for creators.
- Design and analyze A/B tests on feed changes; check for novelty effects.
- Use causal inference methods when experiments are not possible.
- Write SQL in BigQuery and build models in dbt.
- Build dashboards and data visualization that PMs and engineers actually use.

Requirements
- Strong SQL. Python or R for analysis (pandas, statsmodels, or tidyverse).
- Solid statistics: hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, power analysis.
- Familiarity with recommendation systems is a plus.
