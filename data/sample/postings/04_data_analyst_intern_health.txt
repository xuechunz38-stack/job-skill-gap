Data Analyst Intern — Northwind Health Network

Northwind runs 14 outpatient clinics. The analytics team helps operations leaders
understand patient volume, wait times and staffing.

Responsibilities
- Pull and clean data with SQL from our clinical data warehouse.
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau and Power BI for clinic managers.
- Perform statistical analysis on appointment no-show rates.
- Build simple forecasting models for weekly patient volume.
- Document metrics definitions and data quality checks.

Qualifications
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel (pivot tables, lookups) and SQL.
- Experience with R programming or Python for analysis.
- Strong data visualization and communication skills.
- Interest in healthcare operations.
