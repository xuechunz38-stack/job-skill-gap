ranking, pricing suggestions and seller fraud detection.

* Schedule training jobs with Airflow, package models with Docker, and track experiments.
* Analyze offline metrics and help run online experiments on new rankers.

Requirements
* Currently pursuing a BS/MS in CS, statistics, or a related field.
* Solid Python and SQL.
* Understanding of ML fundamentals: bias/variance, regularization, cross-validation.
* Familiarity with deep learning is a plus.
* Experience with AWS (SageMaker or EC2) is a plus.
